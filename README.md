Note: this is actually a re-upload of a hackathon I did with 2 of my friends in 2025. For some reason my friend who originally uploaded the repo took it down, but I put it back together because I had the files saved.

Compete 1 on 1 in a crossword puzzle of your choice. Take turns guessing words, gaining more points for longer words. Be careful, though, because you only have 30 seconds per turn, and your turn ends early with an incorrect guess!
Created for the HackRPI hackathon in fall 2025.
